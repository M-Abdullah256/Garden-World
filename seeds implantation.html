Seed Implantation

A step-by-step guide to planting seeds for your perfect garden!







  • What Is Seed Implantation?

Seed implantation is the process of planting seeds in soil, ensuring the right conditions for germination. Learn the optimal techniques and tools to improve the growth rate of your plants.




Basic Steps for Successful Seed Implantation




Step 1:
Choose the Right Seeds

Start with selecting the right type of seeds suitable for the climate and soil in your garden.

Step 2:
Prepare the Soil

Make sure your soil is fertile and loose. Add compost or organic matter to improve its quality.

Step 3:
Plant the Seeds

Plant your seeds at the correct depth and space, depending on the type of plant.

More Steps




1. Pre-procedure Consultation and Imaging



  • The physician discusses the patient's condition, medical history, and potential treatment options.
  • Imaging techniques like CT scans, MRIs, or ultrasounds are used to precisely locate the tumor and plan the implantation procedure.


2. Patient Preparation



  • The patient is instructed on how to prepare, which may include fasting for a certain period.
  • Anesthesia is administered to minimize discomfort, either general anesthesia or local anesthesia depending on the procedure and location.


3. Marking the Implant Site



  • The physician marks the exact location on the patient’s body where the seeds will be implanted. This is done based on the pre-procedure imaging results.


4. Insertion of the Needle/Applicator



  • A specialized needle or applicator is inserted into the body at the planned implantation site, using the guidance of imaging equipment to ensure precision.


5. Placement of Radioactive Seeds



  • Small radioactive seeds (typically made of iodine-125 or palladium-103) are inserted through the needle or applicator into the tumor or target area.
  • These seeds are usually placed in a grid pattern, based on the specific shape and size of the tumor.
